description: "A five-part investigation into Velotix Ltd, an Israeli cybersecurity startup that marketed angel investments through an ISA-licensed crowdfunding platform while under government enforcement for not paying its employees — and what happened after Part 1 was published."
---

An investigation, in five parts, built on government enforcement findings, court filings, archived pages and public records.

**Disclosure:** I am a former Velotix employee (October 2024 -- May 2025), and I filed the Ministry of Labour complaint referenced throughout. Where I draw on my own experience rather than the documentary record, I say so.

Part 4 documents a fraudulent DMCA notice filed against Part 1 from an address in Chittagong, citing an unrelated Israeli newspaper article. Part 5 covers the company's recovery, as told to *Maariv*.
